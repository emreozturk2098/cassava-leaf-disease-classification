# Original Cassava source archive
Original EE470 Python scripts and notebooks, including the `470_PROJE_SON_HAL_KODLAR!!!!!!` submission folder, are preserved in `source/ee470/`. Working variants remain separately named. Original EE470 reports/deck and CS523 final report, LaTeX tables and bibliography are in `reports/`.

EE470 was undergraduate coursework at Yeditepe; CS523 was graduate coursework at Özyeğin. Emre Öztürk and Ali Baki Türköz contributed equally to both studies. Instructor-supplied EE470 test images were provided separately by email, according to Emre; no unseen test set is invented or inferred from the local training directories.

No training was rerun. Existing reported study results and caveats remain unchanged. The CS523 ZIPs contain report/figure material, not a recovered complete CS523 training pipeline. Original EE470 scripts/notebooks may require path and library adjustments and refer to dataset/model files not bundled here. Public dataset licensing remains with its source.
